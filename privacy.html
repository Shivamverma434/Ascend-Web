Ascend Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Ascend is a personal planner for goals, study, fitness, sleep and journaling. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The developer does not run a server and never receives your data.

What Ascend stores

Everything you enter or connect (goals, journal entries, study logs, check-ins, workouts, sleep and health readings) is saved on your own device. On Windows it is saved in your Google Drive folder if one is present, otherwise in the app's local data folder. The app keeps a few daily backups in the same place.

Sync between your devices

If you turn on sync, Ascend sends your data to a Google Apps Script and a file in your own Google account, which you set up yourself. The developer has no access to it. Google's privacy policy applies to that storage.

Services you choose to connect

Ascend only contacts these when you add your own link, key or profile in Settings:

  • intervals.icu: workouts and wellness data, using your API key.
  • Health Connect (Android only): sleep, heart rate, HRV, steps, workouts, body measurements and nutrition, read with your permission. Ascend does not write to Health Connect.
  • Google Calendar: your calendar's private address, to show events.
  • Notion: the pages you share with your own integration.
  • Goodreads, Chess.com, Lichess: public profile feeds you enter.

Data from these services is only used to show your progress inside the app. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Ascend removes its local data folder. Data in your Google Drive folder or your own Apps Script file stays until you delete it there. You can disconnect any service by clearing it in Settings.

Children

Ascend is not directed at children under 13.

Contact
